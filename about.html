ABOUT ME/

My name is Yaw Okyere Darko and I am 19 years old. I am
passionate about design and visual arts, and I have been
doing computer graphics for about two years. At first, it
was just out of curiosity, but it quickly became a real
passion.

I enjoy creating, learning, and improving a little more
every day. My goal is to turn this passion into a career
and continue to grow in this field that
I truly love.

I have learned how to use several professional creative
and development tools, including Figma, Visual Studio
Code, Blender, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign,
After Effects, and Premiere Pro. These tools have helped
me improve my skills in design, animation, video editing,
and digital creation.
